## About this session

We are testing the design of a web application. It is called the Bitcoin Address Activity Explorer. It shows, for one Bitcoin address, the activity on that address day by day, and it marks some transactions as unusual compared with that address's own earlier activity.

We are testing the design of the application, **not you**. There are no right or wrong answers, and you cannot fail the session.

## What you will do

- You will be asked to complete five short tasks on a laptop or desktop computer. Each task takes a few minutes.
- You will be asked to say out loud what you are thinking as you work.
- The session lasts about 30 minutes.
- A team member (the facilitator) will read the tasks. Another team member (the observer) will write notes. Neither will help you during the tasks. You can ask questions at the end.

## What we will write down

- How long each task takes, and whether you finish it.
- Where you hesitate or make mistakes.
- Your words, as you say them.
- If you agree to the recording, the screen you are looking at (not your face, unless your camera is on; please keep it off).

We will not ask for your address, your date of birth, your student number, your contact details, or any other personal details. The only name we keep is your **first name or initials**, and only on this form.

## What happens to the notes and recording

- We use the notes to improve the design. We write up the results with **no names**. Participants are referred to as P1, P2 and so on.
- If you agreed to a recording, we **delete it once the session has been marked**, which is expected to be within [number] days of the session. We keep the written notes in the team's project folder until the project is assessed, then delete them. The retention period must match Swinburne's rules.
- Only the four team members on this project see the notes and the recording.
